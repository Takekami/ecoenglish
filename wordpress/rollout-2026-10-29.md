## Limits and rollback

Weekly questions reuse existing material rather than independently fact-checking
old lessons. Unsupported question/answer markup is skipped. Weekly refresh is
computed on render; the additional WP-Cron cache refresh relies on WordPress
traffic, not an exact-time external scheduler. No email delivery, paid service,
affiliate publishing or additional AI generation call was added.

To roll back WordPress, install previous plugin code or deactivate it and remove
the new shortcode pages/links. To roll back the Lambda, redeploy its prior image.
Podcast metadata is updated after WordPress confirms its public lesson URL;
services are not transactional. Failures are logged without triggering duplicate
episode uploads. See `docs/podcast-lesson-links.md`.
